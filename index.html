Introduction

UNeeds: Shop What Your Course Needs is an online shopping platform for college students. It lets them browse and buy school-related products such as uniforms, supplies, books and accessories, and filter them by department, course and category.

The idea came from the difficulty of finding department-specific items: students often visit several stores or sift through many listings. UNeeds offers one convenient website with product search, department filtering, a shopping cart, checkout with a simulated PayMongo payment flow, delivery or pickup options, and order tracking.

Objectives

  • Provide an online platform to browse school-related products.
  • Categorize products by college department and course.
  • Let students filter and search for items relevant to their studies.
  • Provide a cart, a simple checkout and order history.
  • Provide an admin system for products, departments, inventory and orders.

Scope and limitations

Students log in with their student number, browse, search, filter, manage a cart, check out and track orders. Admins manage products, prices, stock, categories and order status. Payments are simulated only using PayMongo’s test-mode flow as reference; no real money moves. No nationwide delivery or live courier tracking. Products are limited to what the admin enters.
